# 🎓 NG CGPA

A CGPA and GPA calculator and academic tracker built for Nigerian university students.

## Overview

Nigerian universities don't all grade the same way, some use a 5.0 scale, others 4.0, and a few still run on the legacy 7.0 scale. NG CGPA handles all three, so you can calculate and track your CGPA correctly no matter which system your school uses, instead of doing the math by hand or hunting for a calculator that actually matches your school's grading scale.

It goes beyond a simple calculator too: you can track every semester, see how each course affects your standing, project future CGPA scenarios, and even get help from a built-in academic assistant.

🔗 Live demo: [ng-cgpa.vercel.app](https://ng-cgpa.vercel.app)

## Features

- **Multi-scale support** — works with 5.0, 4.0, and legacy 7.0 grading scales, and lets you select your specific university
- **Full course and semester tracking** — log every course with its credit units, score, and grade, organized by semester, with automatic GP and QP calculation
- **CGPA breakdown** — see your overall CGPA, total credit units, quality points, number of semesters and courses completed, and your honours classification (e.g. Second Class Upper)
- **GPA trend tracking** — visualizes how your GPA has moved across semesters, highlighting your best and weakest terms
- **Smart insights** — flags specific courses that are dragging down your CGPA, especially high-credit courses with low grades, so you know exactly where to focus
- **Grade projections** — set a target CGPA and see what GPA you'd need in your remaining semesters to hit it
- **"What if" calculator** — enter a hypothetical next-semester GPA and credit load to instantly see the impact on your CGPA
- **International scale conversion** — convert your CGPA to an international equivalent (WES) for grad school or immigration purposes
- **Academic Assistant** — a built-in AI chat assistant that can answer questions about your CGPA, help draft an academic appeal letter, estimate if First Class is still achievable, or suggest which courses to prioritize
- **Import/Export** — import courses by pasting text or CSV, restore a saved session from JSON, and export your results as a PDF report, JSON, or plain text summary, or share directly to WhatsApp

## Tech Stack

Built with **React** and **Vite** for a fast, modern front-end experience.

## Why I Built It

Most CGPA calculators online are built around US or UK grading systems and don't map cleanly onto how Nigerian universities actually grade. NG CGPA was built to close that gap, and to go a step further than a plain calculator, giving students a real sense of academic standing, where their CGPA is heading, and what it would take to hit their goals.
